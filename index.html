Why these exist

Most freelance leaks are not in the craft. They are in the emails around it: a vague proposal, a free "small add," a soft invoice follow-up, a project that never quite closes.

Each skill here is a repeatable procedure for one of those jobs. It asks for the client's words, the agreed scope, and your rates. If a number is missing, it leaves a placeholder. It will not invent a testimonial, a fee, or a yes you did not give.

A real preview, from the skill's own example

Client, mid-project: "Can we also add a gift-card page before launch? Should be quick if we reuse the subscription layout." The agreed site is 6 named pages. Extra pages were excluded. Freelancer extra-work rate: $150/hour.

CLASSIFICATION
Request, in one sentence: Add a gift-card page before the 15 April launch, reusing the subscription layout.
Class: out of scope
Reason: The accepted 26 November proposal lists 6 named pages and excludes extra pages and shop work.

IMPACT
Time: 12 to 16 hours, if we only build a page that links out to an existing Toast gift-card URL.
Cost: $1,800 USD fixed.
Deadline: 15 April can hold if you approve this week and send the link and copy within 3 business days.

OPTIONS
A. Paid change order: $1,800, work starts after written approval and a $900 deposit.
B. Park the gift-card page until after launch.
C. Swap: drop the About page (not yet built) and build Gift Cards instead, same fee, same date.

EMAIL DRAFT
Subject: Gift-card page, scope and estimate

Hi Priya,

A gift-card page is new work, so it sits outside the 6 pages in the approved proposal. I can still do it before 15 April if we treat it as a written change.

What I would ship: a Gift Cards page in the current design, with a button that opens your existing Toast gift-card link, copy you send, and mobile QA.

Fee: $1,800 USD, with $900 to start and $900 when the page is on staging. The 15 April launch date holds if you approve this week and I have the working Toast link plus your copy within 3 business days.

If that link is not live yet, I recommend we launch the 6 pages we already agreed and I quote the gift-card page as a follow-on, rather than tying the launch to Toast setup I cannot control.

Reply with A (paid change), B (park it), or C (swap About). I will not start the page until I have that reply.

Jordan
