# Free freelance late-invoice email scripts

Copy these when an invoice is due, overdue, or a client has gone quiet on payment. Each script is ready to send. Fill in the bracketed bits, keep the tone calm and specific, and do not invent late fees, pause rights, or legal threats that are not already in your agreed terms.

These are templates, not legal, tax, or collections advice. Match them to your contract, your currency, and what you have already sent. The aim is to get paid or to get a clear date, not to perform anger.

## How to use these scripts

1. Put the invoice number, amount, original due date, and one payment method in the first screen of the email.
2. Count days overdue from the due date, not from the invoice date, unless they are the same.
3. Choose the step that matches the facts, not your mood. If you already sent a reminder, skip ahead.
4. Offer one simple way to pay. Do not list five.
5. If the client raised a genuine dispute, separate "please pay the undisputed part" from "let's resolve this item." Do not keep chasing the full amount as if they said nothing.
6. Leave out sarcasm, apologies for invoicing, threats of lawyers or public exposure, and stories about your cash flow. "I close the books on [date]" is enough if you need a reason for a deadline.
7. Do not add late fees, interest, or admin charges unless they are already in the agreed terms. If they are, quote the clause in plain language and show the math.

Subject lines are suggestions. On Slack or WhatsApp, skip the subject and send a tightened version of the body. A short Slack version is at the end.

## 1. Courtesy nudge, due tomorrow or due today

Use only if you want a light reminder before the invoice is late. Skip this if you already sent one, or if the client is already overdue.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] is due [tomorrow, 27 April]

> Hi [Priya],
>
> A quick note that invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD, the remaining balance on the [Northline] site after staging sign-off, is due [tomorrow, 27 April].
>
> You can pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> If it is already on its way, please ignore this. If anything on the invoice looks wrong, tell me what to fix.
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], $[3,600] USD

## 2. First reminder, 1 to 7 days overdue

Assume it was missed. Be specific, not heavy.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], $[3,600], was due [27 April]

> Hi [Priya],
>
> Invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD was due on [27 April] and is now [4] days overdue. This is the remaining balance on the [Northline] site, issued after staging sign-off on [20 April].
>
> Please pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> If you have already paid, send the receipt and I will check. If something is wrong with the invoice, tell me what to fix.
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], $[3,600] USD

---

## 3. Second reminder, 8 to 14 days overdue

Restate the amount, the due date, and the payment method. Ask for a payment date this week.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], $[3,600] due, need a payment date

> Hi [Priya],
>
> Invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD was due on [27 April] and is now [14] days overdue. I sent the invoice on [20 April] and a reminder on [4 May], and I have not had a reply.
>
> Please pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> If you have already paid, send the receipt. If there is a problem with the invoice, tell me what to fix. Otherwise, please confirm a payment date this week.
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], $[3,600] USD

---

## 4. Delivery is ready and waiting on payment

Use when the work is done, or a handover, transfer, or launch is being held because the terms say it waits on this balance.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], $[3,600] due, [site transfer] on hold

> Hi [Priya],

> The site is ready to transfer to your [Webflow] account. I will transfer it and help with launch as soon as this payment clears, as set out in the proposal.
>
> Please pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> If you have already paid, send the receipt and I will check. If something is wrong with the invoice, tell me what to fix. Otherwise, please confirm a payment date this week.
>
> [Jordan]

## 5. Firm reminder, and a pause date if the contract allows it

Use at about 15 to 21 days overdue, when work is still in progress and your agreement lets you pause. If the terms do not allow a pause, use script 3 again, or script 8, and do not invent a pause right.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], need a payment date by [21 May]

> Hi [Priya],
>
> Invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD was due on [27 April] and is now [18] days overdue. I have not had a payment or a reply to the reminders of [4 May] and [11 May].
>
> Please pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> If the balance has not arrived, and I have not heard about a problem with the invoice, I will pause [remaining build work] on [21 May], as the agreement allows. I can restart as soon as payment clears.
>
> If you have already paid, send the receipt. If something is wrong, tell me what to fix. Otherwise, please confirm a payment date by [21 May].
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], $[3,600] USD

## 6. They promised a date and missed it

Name the promised date once. Ask for a new date or payment this week. Do not pile on.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], following up on the [16 May] payment

> Hi [Priya],
>
> On [12 May] you said invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD would be paid on [16 May]. I have not seen the payment yet.
>
> Please pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> If it has already gone out, send the receipt and I will check. If you need a new date, please send one for this week.
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], $[3,600] USD, original due date [27 April]

---

## 7. They disputed part of the invoice

Do not chase the full amount as if nothing was said. Ask them to pay the undisputed part, and deal with the rest in writing.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], pay the agreed $[2,400], and let's close the $[1,200] item

> Hi [Priya],
>
> Thank you for flagging the $[1,200] line for the [extra photography round] on invoice [NL-014]. I do not want that item to hold up the rest.
>
> Please pay the undisputed $[2,400] USD now: [PAYMENT_LINK]
>
> For the remaining $[1,200], I will send a short note with what was in the approved proposal and what was added later, and we can close that item separately. If you would rather jump on a 15-minute call, suggest a time this week.
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], total $[3,600], undisputed $[2,400]

## 8. Final notice, 22 days or more overdue

Stay factual. Do not threaten lawyers, collections, or public exposure. If you want a formal collections path, speak to a professional in the client's jurisdiction. This script stops at a firm notice and, if your terms allow it, a pause or withheld delivery.

Subject: Final notice, invoice [NL-014], $[3,600], due [27 April]

> Hi [Priya],
>
> Invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD was due on [27 April] and is now [24] days overdue. I sent the invoice on [20 April] and reminders on [4 May], [11 May], and [18 May], and I have not received payment or a written dispute.
>
> Please pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]
> Or use the bank details on the invoice.
>
> I need payment, a receipt, or a written note of what is wrong by [29 May]. If I do not have one of those, [the site transfer and launch stay on hold / remaining work stays paused], as set out in the proposal, and I will not schedule further work on this project until the balance is cleared.
>
> If you have already paid, send the receipt today and I will check.
>
> [Jordan]
> Invoice: [NL-014], [20 April 2027], $[3,600] USD

---

## 9. They say they already paid

Do not argue. Ask for the receipt, say when you will check, and keep the invoice open until it is confirmed.

Subject: Invoice [NL-014], sending the receipt so I can check

> Hi [Priya],
>
> Thank you. I have not seen invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] USD land yet.
>
> Please forward the receipt, payment confirmation, or transaction ID, including the date and the account it was sent to. I will check this afternoon and confirm as soon as it shows up.
>
> If the payment was sent to a different account or under a different reference, tell me what to look for.
>
> [Jordan]

## 10. Short Slack or WhatsApp versions

First reminder, 1 to 7 days overdue:

> Hi [Priya], invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] was due [27 April] and is now [4] days overdue. Pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]. If it is already paid, please send the receipt. If anything looks wrong, tell me what to fix.

Firm note, around 15 to 21 days, only if the terms allow a pause:

> Hi [Priya], invoice [NL-014] for $[3,600] is now [18] days overdue. I still need a payment or a payment date by [21 May]. Pay here: [PAYMENT_LINK]. If it has not arrived by then, I will pause [remaining build work] until it clears, as the agreement allows.
